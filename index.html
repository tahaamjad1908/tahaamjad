Profile

Consulting first, then in-house.

I started at Akhtar & Hasan Actuaries, running IAS 19 employee benefit valuations for clients across Pakistan and the GCC and building a reserving model for an insurance client. I moved to State Life Insurance Corporation of Pakistan in 2025, where I took Group Takaful pricing end to end and owned valuation, IBNR and Financial Condition Reporting for the Takaful portfolios.

Since June 2026 I review the team's pricing, valuation and reporting work rather than only producing it, and I lead the actuarial models and automated tools those processes run on. The automation is the part I care about most: a valuation that runs itself leaves room to think about whether the answer is right.

Alongside the day job I've cleared eleven SOA exams and modules, finishing the preliminary series and all three Corporate Finance & ERM track modules.
